Zeb is an award-winning filmmaker with a background in scientific research, having worked on a range of fiction, documentary, and experimental projects.

He was recently awarded the Best Micro Short award at the BFI Future Film Festival for his film [36,000 Words for Love](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pZEWuO9X8ME&t=2s).

In 2024, he was the Festival Director for the 15th edition of [Watersprite Film Festival](https://watersprite.org.uk/). With a team of 60 students, he delivered a full weekend of free talks, screenings, and ceremonies celebrating the future of film and breaking down the barriers for those pursuing a career in the creative industries.

He recently completed a PhD in Computational Linguistics, exploring whether training AI models with sounds (instead of written language) can teach us something about how children first acquire language.
