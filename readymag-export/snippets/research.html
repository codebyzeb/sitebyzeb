WORK

Zeb studied for a Ph.D. in Computer Science at the University of Cambridge from 2021 to 2025. He was supervised by Professor Paula Buttery and funded by the EPSRC Vice-Chancellor Scholarship.


His thesis, titled "Phonological Representations in Language Models", explores the training of small language models with sound-based units (phonemes) instead of text-based units (characters or words). This work draws parallels between modern machine learning methods and statistical learning theories of language acquisition, leading to new insights across both fields.


He was a Research Scientist Intern at Spotify in 2026, studying large-scale entity resolution and LLM-based methods for augmenting labelled data.
