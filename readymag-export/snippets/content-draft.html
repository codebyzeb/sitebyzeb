Zeb has been posting "content" online since he started his YouTube channel FilmByZeb in 2016. Originally posting simple travel vlogs, he used the channel as a place to document his life, learn filmmaking, and experiment creatively. He has since posted over 100 videos, including several award-winning short films.


Zeb also produces short-form content across multiple platforms. He uses these videos to test creative ideas and methods for storytelling. In 2022, his TikTok on Wordle went viral, reaching over one million views.


His recent short-form series The PhD Grind documented the final year of his PhD using comedy, experimental editing, and animated characters. He is currently in pre-production for a new series about creativity.

Zeb posts his first YouTube video, a simple montage of a family hiking trip. Inspired by Casey Neistat and Sara Dietschy, he edits five vlogs from a later trip to New York. Teaching himself to edit, it takes him seven months to make these five videos.
